About STEMFest

STEMFest Robotics Expo brings together young innovators, engineers, and creators to showcase their projects and compete in hands-on challenges.

Expect interactive exhibits, mentorship sessions, and competitions across robotics platforms. Open to students, educators, families, and industry partners.
